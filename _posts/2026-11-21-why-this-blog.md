Reels are good for one idea at a time. Some ideas need more room: where they come from, where they apply, and where they stop applying.

This blog is for those. I'll write about Indian philosophy, psychology and inner work the same way I make videos: from what I've tried and observed, and with the source named whenever I'm borrowing from a tradition or a study.

Some posts will be in English, some in Hinglish.
