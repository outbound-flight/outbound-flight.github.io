My story

Nine years a software developer. IIT Kharagpur, Computer Science.

I started as a skeptic.

I wanted evidence and a mechanism before believing anything.

I tried a lot: meditation, breathwork, mantra, devotional practice, therapy, yoga.

When something worked, I didn't just accept the explanation I was given. I wanted to know why. The common thread I kept finding was emotion.

I left software to teach and create.

Trained as a yoga teacher (Yogarambha RYS300). From a language-activism background, so I care about getting Sanskrit right. I speak Hinglish because that's how I think.

I speak from what I've observed, not as an authority.

I try to say where an idea comes from: a tradition, a study, or just my own practice.

This is not therapy and not a replacement for it. If you're dealing with something heavy, please speak to a qualified professional.
